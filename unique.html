What Makes Us Unique?

Regular passkey and biometric authentication methods are basic and don't innovate. Some people aren't satisfied with a single method of authentication, and we wanted to create something to solve that. Emotilock is the first face ID recognition system that uses a sequence of your emotions to unlock your device. This gives it an extra layer of security that ensures the maximum security and privacy for the user.
